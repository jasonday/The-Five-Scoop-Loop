## Core Challenge Rules
**The goal:** Run a loop that visits five ice cream shops, eat one serving at each, and return to your starting shop — all in a single continuous activity.

### 🧡 Spirit of the Challenge
The Five Scoop Loop has a lot of rules because we want every runner to have a fair shot at the leaderboard — but at its core, this challenge is meant to be fun. You’re running, exploring your community, eating ice cream, and probably questioning your life choices somewhere around scoop four. Enjoy it. Laugh. Take silly photos. Celebrate the absurdity.

Whether you’re chasing the leaderboard or just out for a joyful sugar‑powered adventure, have fun out there.

### Eligibility Requirements
* You may start at any of the ice cream shops on the loop. 
* You must visit five shops total (including your start/end point).
* You must return to your starting shop to complete the challenge.
* Your run must be recorded as one continuous GPS activity. Manual pauses, auto‑pause, device switching, or suspicious GPS jumps may disqualify a leaderboard attempt.

### Ice Cream Requirements
* Minimum: one scoop (generally, a “small”) or equivalent dairy-free/vegan option.
* Ice cream must be purchased on-site — no ordering ahead.
* You may not cut in line or otherwise disrupt normal customer flow.
* Ice cream must be *completely* consumed *on premises* — meaning the shop’s seating area, patio, or designated outdoor space. Eating while walking away does not count.
* No sharing the required serving between multiple runners.
* If a shop offers only soft‑serve or only hard‑serve, either is acceptable.

### Proof Requirements
* Take a photo or video at each shop showing you eating the ice cream
* Photos or videos must be taken during the run and must clearly show the shop name or identifiable features.

### Submit:
* A public activity link (Strava, Garmin, etc.)
* Your five photos, or a link to an Instagram post or YouTube video containing your evidence. 
* A GPX file
* Optional: a selfie at the final shop to celebrate your survival
* If running as a group, each runner must submit their own activity link and photos.
* Submit here: [https://forms.gle/iGAL5YutMu5bbtK39](https://forms.gle/iGAL5YutMu5bbtK39)

### Route Requirements
* You may choose any order of shops and run any legal pedestrian route (roads, sidewalks, trails).
* Total distance will vary depending on your chosen path — strategy matters.
* If you choose to consume your ice cream at the start (at your starting shop) rather than at the end, you must start your GPS recording before entering the line. Typically this means starting your watch on the sidewalk or outside the shop — the same place you will finish your loop.
* If you choose to consume your ice cream at the end (at your starting shop), you must not stop your GPS recording until you have completely consumed the ice cream. 

### Safety & Conduct
* Follow all traffic laws and run safely.
* Respect shop staff and customers.
* Clean up any trash (cups, spoons, napkins).
* Be mindful of shop hours. Arriving before opening or after closing invalidates that stop.
* If a shop is extremely crowded, be patient — do not pressure staff or customers.

### Leaderboard vs. Fun Runs
* The leaderboard ranks completed loops by finish time, fastest first.
* Leaderboard runs must use the same five designated shops for that region. Any substitution automatically moves the run to the Fun Run board.

### Leaderboard integrity
* Leaderboard times may be audited. Submissions with inconsistent GPS data, missing photos, or unclear evidence may be rejected or moved to Fun Run status.
