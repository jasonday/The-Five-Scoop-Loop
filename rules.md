## Core Challenge Rules
The goal: Run a loop that visits five ice cream shops, eat ice cream at each one, and return to your starting shop — all in a single continuous activity.

### Eligibility Requirements
* You may start at any participating ice cream shop.
* You must visit five shops total (including your start/end point).
* You must return to your starting shop to complete the challenge.
* The run must be one continuous activity — no pausing to drive, bike, or teleport. Your must not pause your watch/GPS tracker at any point. 

### Ice Cream Requirements
* Minimum: one scoop (generally, a “small”) or equivalent dairy-free option.
* Ice cream must be purchased on-site — no ordering ahead.
* Ice cream must be consumed on premises before running to the next shop.
* No sharing one scoop between multiple runners.

### Proof Requirements
* Take a photo at each shop showing:
  * You or your hand holding the ice cream with something identifiable from the shop location (sign, counter, exterior, etc.)


### Submit:
* A public activity link (Strava, Garmin, etc.)
* Your five photos
* A GPX file
* Optional: a selfie at the final shop to celebrate your survival
* Submit here: [https://forms.gle/iGAL5YutMu5bbtK39](https://forms.gle/iGAL5YutMu5bbtK39)

### Route Requirements
* You may choose any order of shops.
* You may run roads, sidewalks, trails, or any legal pedestrian route.
* Total distance will vary depending on your chosen path — strategy matters.

### Safety & Conduct
* Follow all traffic laws and run safely.
* Respect shop staff and customers.
* Clean up any trash (cups, spoons, napkins).
* If a shop is unexpectedly closed, you may reroute to another — but you still need five total. If an alternative was chosen, indicate that in the submission form. 

### Leaderboard vs. Fun Runs
The leaderboard ranks completed loops by finish time, fastest first.

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
**Did a closed shop force you to reroute?** If you answered yes on the submission
form, your run is logged as a **fun run** rather than a ranked result. Fun runs
get their own board and are still celebrated. They are kept separate so every
ranked runner is measured against the same five stops.
</div>
